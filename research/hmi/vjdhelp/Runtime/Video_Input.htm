Video Input

Menu Items

Description

Signal

NTSC and PAL are the two standard video signals supported. Make sure the video signal you select matches your camera's video signal.

Brightness

Controls the brightness of the video.

Settings range from 0 to 127.

Default value is 0.

Contrast

Controls the contrast of the video.

Settings range from 0 to 7.

Chroma U

Controls the digital gain to the U (or Cb) component of the digital video signal.

Settings range from 0 to 255.

Chroma V

Controls the digital gain to the V (or Cr) component of the digital video signal.

Settings range from 0 to 255.

Hue

Controls the color hue.

Settings range from 0 to 127.

Sharpness

Controls the sharpness.

Settings range from 0 to 15.
