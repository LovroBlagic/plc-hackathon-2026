18.9.1.8 Video Methods

Use the following methods to interact with Video Display. For more information on Video, see Section 19.1.1, About Video.

Method

Description

setFrame

Moves the video playback to the defined location.

toggleVideoInput

Toggles the Video Display mode from display live feed to play file or from play file to display live feed.

stop

Stops the live video feed or stops the video file.

play

Displays live video feed or plays the video file.

pause

Pauses the live video feed or pauses the video file.

record

Records the live video feed.

fastForward

Fasts forward the video file.

rewind

Rewinds the video file.

snapshot

Takes a snapshot of the video.
