18.8.4 Cable Connections

Connect the serial equipment to the target machine COM port as defined in the Driver Configuration dialog box.

You can connect the serial equipment using RS-232C, RS-422 (2-wire), or RS-422 (4-wire), depending on what signals are used by your serial equipment. See Appendix 5.4.1, Script Driver Cable Connections, for descriptions of the script driver cable connections.

For the Panel W Series, Panel Universal Series, and Panel Performance Series of target machines that are equipped with an IF option (RS-232/RS-422/RS-485 interface module), plug in to Slot 1 and select COM Port 4 in the Driver Configuration dialog box. COM Port 3 is reserved for internal functions.
