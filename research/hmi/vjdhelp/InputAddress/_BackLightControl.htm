_BackLight.Control

Integer variable that turns the backlight ON or OFF. You can also set the backlight to turn OFF automatically after a specified period of inactivity.

 

ControllerStatus Value

Backlight State

0

Default value; backlight does nothing.

-1

Backlight turns ON. Countdown is triggered.

-2

Turns backlight OFF. When the panel is touched, backlight turns ON and stays ON. Control remains -2.

Greater than 0

Turns backlight off after specified number of minutes of inactivity. Valid values are 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, and 30 minutes. Other numbers entered are rounded down to the closest valid value. For example, 3 is rounded down to 2. 9 is rounded down to 5. Any value greater than 30 will be rounded down to 30 minutes.

  •  Countdown does not restart  when the specified minutes of inactivity is changed.
