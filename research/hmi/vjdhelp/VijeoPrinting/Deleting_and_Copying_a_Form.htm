27.3.3 Cutting, Copying, and Pasting Forms

Deleting Forms

To delete a form:

  1. In the Navigator window, select the form you want to remove under the Forms & Reports node.

  2. Select Delete Form from the right-click menu.

To delete all the forms:

  1. In the Navigator window, select the Forms & Reports node.

  2. Select Delete All Forms from the right-click menu.

Copying Forms

You can copy a form from one target to another or from one project to another by opening two instances of Vijeo-Designer.

To copy and paste a form:

  1. In the Navigator window, select the form you want to copy under the Forms & Reports node.

  2. Select Copy Form from the right-click menu.

  3. Select the Forms & Reports node in the target you want to copy the form to, and select Paste Form from the right-click menu.

  • Check that the printer control codes used in the form are defined in the printer settings for the target you have copied the form to.

Cut and Paste Forms

You can move a form from one target to another or from one project to another by opening two instances of Vijeo-Designer.

To cut and paste a form:

  1. In the Navigator window, select the form you want to copy under the Forms & Reports node.

  2. Select Cut Form from the right-click menu.

  3. Select the Forms & Reports node in the target you want to paste the form to, and select Paste Form from the right-click menu.
