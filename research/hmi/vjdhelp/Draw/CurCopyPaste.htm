7.4.4 Cutting/Copying/Pasting/Deleting Objects

The following operations can also be performed with their corresponding Edit menu items or their Edit toolbar icons.

Cutting

Right-click an object and click Cut to move the selected object to the clipboard.

Copying

Right-click an object and click Copy to move a copy of the selected object to the clipboard.

Pasting

  1. Cut or copy a selection of one or more objects to the clipboard.

  2. Right-click the panel and click Paste to move the object from the clipboard to the panel.

  1. Click outside the object's selection box to set the object's location on the panel.

Deleting

Right-click an object and click Delete to remove the selected object from the panel.
