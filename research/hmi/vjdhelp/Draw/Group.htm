7.4.6 Grouping/Ungrouping Objects

The Group and Ungroup operations can be performed with their corresponding Arrange menu items or their Arrange toolbar icons.

Grouping

  1. Select two or more objects that you want to group together as a single object. See Section 7.4.2, Refining Selections.

  1. Right-click any one of the objects within the selection box, then point to Group and click Group.

All objects are grouped together in one selection box.

Ungrouping

  1. Select a grouped object that you want to ungroup.

  1. Right-click any one of the objects within the selection box, then point to Group and click Ungroup.

The objects are ungrouped, and each is displayed in its individual selection box.
