7.4.8 Rotating/Flipping Objects

The following operations can also be performed with their corresponding Arrange menu items or their Arrange toolbar icons.

Rotate Left

  1. Select and right-click an object that you want to rotate to the left (counterclockwise).

  1. In the right-click menu, point to Rotate/Flip and click Rotate Left.

The selected object rotates 90 degrees to the left (counterclockwise).

Rotate Right

  1. Select and right-click an object that you want to rotate to the right (clockwise).

  1. In the right-click menu, point to Rotate/Flip and click Rotate Right.

The selected object rotates 90 degrees to the right (clockwise).

Flip Horizontal

When you use the Flip Horizontal operation, the object flips its right and left sides, but its position, location, and the size of its selection box do not change.

  1. Select and right-click an object that you want to flip horizontally (from side to side).

  1. In the right-click menu, point to Rotate/Flip and click Flip Horizontal.

The selected object flips its sides horizontally (on its Y axis).

Flip Vertical

When you use the Flip Vertical operation, the object flips vertically (upside-down), but its position, location, and the size of its selection box do not change.

  1. Select and right-click an object that you want to flip vertically (upside-down).

  1. In the right-click menu, point to Rotate/Flip and click Flip Vertical.

The selected object flips vertically, upside-down (on its X axis).
