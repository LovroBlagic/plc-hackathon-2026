4.14 Working With E-mail

Vijeo Designer E-mail can send e-mail notifications to inform users of target machine operations. The E-mail's body can either be a text message or a Vijeo Designer Form. Forms enable you to E-mail current variable values, current alarm statuses, current recipe values, etc. E-mail text messages can contain regular text, current variable values, and time stamps. E-mails can also have alarm files, recipe files, data logging files, screen snapshots, and video files attached to them.

E-mail notifications can be triggered by Actions, Switches, Function Keys, and Selectors.

You can also send Email or SMS messages to your tablet/smartphone with a link to open the Vijeo Design’Air Plus App and launch the alarm page. For more information, see Accessing the Alarm Panel from Email or SMS message in Section 23.1.1.3 Working in Vijeo Design’Air Plus.

 

E-mail Requirements

  • Target Machine Supports Ethernet

  • Target Machine has access to a SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) Server
