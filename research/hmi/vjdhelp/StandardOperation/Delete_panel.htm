4.7.3.2 Deleting a Panel

Deleting a Specific Panel

You can delete a panel that resides under a Base Panels or Master Panels node, in a folder, or in a sub-folder.

  1. In the Project tab of the Navigator window, right-click the panel you want to delete and select Delete Panel.

  1. Alternatively, you can select the panel and use the Delete key.

  1. In the Delete Panel confirmation message, click Yes.

The selected panel is deleted.

 

Deleting All Panels

You can delete all panels that reside under the Base Panels or Master Panels node or in a folder.  

To delete all panels under the Base Panels or Master Panels node:

  1. In the Navigator Window's Project tab, right-click the Base Panels or Master Panels node, and select Delete All Panels.

  2. Confirm the delete operation by clicking Yes. All the panels that reside under the Base Panels or Master Panels node are deleted. It does not remove the panels that are organized in the folders and sub-folders.

To delete all panels in folders and sub-folders:

  1. In the Navigator Window's Project tab, right-click the folder or sub-folder under the Base Panels or Master Panels node, and select Delete All Panels.

  2. Confirm the delete operation by clicking Yes. All the panels that reside under the folder or sub-folder are deleted.
