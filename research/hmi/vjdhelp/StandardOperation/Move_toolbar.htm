4.10.2.5 Moving a Toolbar

You can move a toolbar by clicking the title bar and dragging it to the area you want.

While a toolbar is being dragged, it is shown as a rectangle. The appearance of the rectangle indicates whether the toolbar is in fixed mode or floating mode after the move.

  • A toolbar moved to the window area will switch automatically to a floating toolbar.

  • If you move a toolbar while holding down the CTRL key, it becomes a floating toolbar after the move.
