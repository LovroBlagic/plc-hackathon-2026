4.9.5 Re-centering the Drawing Area

When working with the panel editor, you may be disoriented about your location in the panel. This occurs most often when you are either zoomed-in or zoomed-out.

Vijeo-Designer offers two commands to help you navigate the panel area.

  • View Center

  • View Top Left

These commands are accessible from either the View menu or when you right-click the panel drawing area.

View menu

Panel Right-Click menu

Example

In the following example, we've zoomed in and have been busy drawing one section of the panel. To get back to the top-left corner of the panel, we could use the horizontal and vertical scrollbars, or we could use the View Top Left command.

  1. Right-click on a blank area of the panel then click View Top Left.
    Make sure you click the panel area itself. If you right-click on an object, the object's edit menu will display.

The panel editor now displays the top-left corner of the drawing area.
