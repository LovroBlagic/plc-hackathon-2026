4.14.6 E-mail Troubleshooting

This section addresses some of the E-mail problems that you may encounter:

  • E-mail to multiple recipients

  • Failing to connect to the E-mail Server

  • Too many E-mails in Queue

  • Long E-mail Messages

 

E-mail to multiple recipients

You can E-mail multiple recipients by using multiple E-mail operations. Multiple E-mails are sent in the order they are received.

 

Failing to connect to the E-mail Server

Vijeo Designer E-mail will attempt to connect to the SMTP server the number of times you have specified in the Retry Count field. If the number of maximum attempts is reached, the E-mail is discarded.

 

Too many E-mails in Queue

Vijeo Designer queues a maximum of 16 E-mails. If the queue is full, new E-mails will not be sent until some of the queue is cleared.

 

Long E-mail Messages

E-mails with messages that are longer than 1000 characters will be truncated.
