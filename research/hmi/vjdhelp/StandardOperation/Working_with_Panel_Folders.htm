4.7.1.2 Working with Panel Folders

To organize your panels into a folder structure, you can create folders and sub-folders, up to a maximum of five folder levels. Using the drag and drop method, panels can be copied or moved between the panel folders and master folders. You can also store individual panels in the Toolchest and reuse them later. See Section 21.2, Using the Toolchest to Store and Reuse Common Objects.

Panel folders support the following operations:

  • Creating new panels in the folder.

  • Creating new sub-folders in the folder.

  • Renaming a folder.

  • Copying and pasting folders in the Base Panels into either its folders or Master Panels folders.

  • Copying and pasting panels into folders.

  • Deleting all panels in a folder.

  • Deleting a folder. The current folder and all its contents (includes panels and sub-folders) will also be deleted.

  • Deleting all folders. All the sub-folders and their contents will be deleted.
