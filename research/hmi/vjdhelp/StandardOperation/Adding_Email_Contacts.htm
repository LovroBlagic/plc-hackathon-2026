4.14.2 Adding E-mail Contacts

E-mail contacts are added through the Vijeo Designer E-mail dialog box.

To add a new E-mail contact:

  1. In the target properties, enable E-mail and click the ellipsis button.

  2. In the E-mail dialog box, click the Add button to create a new contact. You can enter up to 15 E-mail contacts.

  3. Enter in the new contact's Alias.

  4. Enter in the new contact's E-mail Address. You can enter one or more e-mail addresses for the E-mail contact, separated by semi-colons, up to a maximum of 100 characters. To send SMS, use a SMS Gateway address.

  5. Click OK to add the new contact.

For information on defining E-mail content, see section 4.14.3, Creating E-mail Content.
