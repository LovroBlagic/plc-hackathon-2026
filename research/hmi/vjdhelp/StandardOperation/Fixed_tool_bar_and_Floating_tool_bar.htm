4.10.2.4 Fixed Toolbars and Floating Toolbars

Fixed Toolbars

Fixed toolbars are displayed embedded in the Vijeo-Designer editor.

Floating Toolbars

Floating toolbars can be displayed on top of Vijeo-Designer editor.

  • To switch a fixed toolbar to a floating toolbar, double-click the toolbar's embedded title bar.

  • To switch a floating toolbar to a fixed toolbar, double-click the toolbar's title bar.
