4.7.3 Copying or Deleting a Panel

4.7.3.1 Copying a Panel

  1. In the Navigator Window's Project tab, right-click the panel you want to copy and select Copy Panel.

  1. Right-click a Base Panels node, Master Panels node, panel folder or popup window node, and select Paste Panel.

The selected panel, including drawings and property settings, is copied to the panel folder. To make more than one copy, repeat Step 2.
