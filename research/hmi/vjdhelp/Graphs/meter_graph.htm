12.2 Meter

What is a Meter?

A Meter is a circular scale with a rotating Indicator in the center of the scale. A Meter links to a variable or an expression. When the value of the variable or expression increases or decreases, the Indicator rotates along the scale with the changing value. The Meter's scales and labels enable you to identify the current value of the variable or expression by the Indicator pointing to the appropriate place on the Meter.
