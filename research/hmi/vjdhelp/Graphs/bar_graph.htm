12.3 Bar Graph

What is a Bar Graph?

A Bar Graph is a rectangular scale with fill animation. A Bar Graph links to a variable or an expression. When the value of the variable or expression increases or decreases, the size of the Indicator increases or decreases along with the changing value. The Bar Graph's scale and labels enable you to measure the length of the Indicator and identify the current value of the variable or the expression that is linked to the Bar Graph. Thin Bar Graphs

You can create a thin bar graph if you need a bar graph smaller than 40 pixels wide for a vertical bar graph, or 40 pixels high for a horizontal bar graph. A thin bar graph is too narrow to display scales and markers, so those features must be turned off. To create a thin bar graph, the plate style must be 000000, the indicator style must be 100% and the scale and threshold markers must be turned off. See Section 12.3.2.1 General Tab for restrictions on creating a thin bar graph.
