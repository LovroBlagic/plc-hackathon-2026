12.2.1 Setting Up a Meter

This section describes the steps to create a Meter.

  • The Meter cannot be drawn less than 20 pixels by 20 pixels.

  1. Click the Meter icon in the Graphical Objects Parts toolbar.

  1. On the panel, click and draw the Meter and then click again to complete the drawing and display the Meter settings.

  1. Configure the Meter settings as required for your project. See Section 12.2.2, Meter Settings, for a list and descriptions of the Meter Settings tabs.
