12.3.1 Setting Up a Bar Graph

This section describes the steps to create a Bar Graph.

  • Horizontal Bar Graphs drawn less than 40 pixels high and Vertical Bar Graphs drawn less than 40 pixels wide are converted automatically to Thin Bar Graphs.

  • Horizontal Thin Bar Graphs must be at least 2 pixels wide by 1 pixel high. Vertical Thin Bar Graphs must be at least 1 pixel wide by 2 pixels high. See Section 12.3.2.1 General Tab for restrictions on creating a Thin Bar Graph.

  1. Click the Bar Graph icon in the Graphical Objects Parts toolbar.

  1. On the panel, click and draw the Bar Graph and then click again to display the Bar Graph settings.

  1. Configure the Bar Graph settings as required for your project. See Section 12.3.2 Bar Graph Settings for a list and descriptions of the Bar Graph Settings tabs.
