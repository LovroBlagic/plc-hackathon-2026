12.1 Types of Graphs

Vijeo-Designer provides you with different types of graphs that you can use to measure the value of a variable or an expression. The following types of graphs are described in this chapter.

  • Parts
    The Meter and Bar Graph Parts have customizable features that can easily be modified to fit the requirements of your projects.

  • Meter

  • Bar Graph

  • Toolchest Graphs
    Toolchest graphs are ready-to-use graphs that only require a little setup. You can drag and drop Toolchest graphs into your project. The following Toolchest graphs are available in the Toolchest:

  • Bar Graph

  • Circle Graph

  • Tank Graph

  • Meter Graph

  • Pie Graph

  • Data Graph

  • Parison Graph

  • XY Graph

  • User Created Graphs (Animation)
    You can animate the graphical objects you draw on a panel to create your own graphs. See Section 12.5, Creating Your Own Graphs.
