Warning 2087: [Alarm Group] [alarm group name] [property name] [variable name] is invalid. Variable already in use.

The variable in the specified property is already being used as a counting variable in an alarm group.

Variables in the AlarmCurCount and AlarmSumCount properties must be unique, as they count the number of active alarms and the total number of activated alarms in an alarm group.

Solution:

  • Enter a unique variable.
