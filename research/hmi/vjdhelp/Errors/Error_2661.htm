Error 2661: [object type] [object name] scale area is too small to draw correctly. To prevent this error, you can decrease the scale's width, (increase the scale's length,) or increase the size of the drawing.

The combined Scale Width and Length settings produce tick marks that are too thick and too short to draw correctly.

Solution:

  • You can increase the scale area by either increasing the scale length or increasing the size of the drawing.

  • Another option is to decrease the scale width.
