Error 2760: [%s] [%s] [%s] is disabled or not supported in the target.

The target's Screen Snapshot or Video property is set to Disabled, or the Screen Snapshot or Video feature is not supported on the target.

Solution:

  • Set the target's Screen Snapshot or Video property to Enabled. Your target may not support the Screen Snapshot or Video feature. For more information on which target supports the Screen Snapshot or Video feature, see Appendix 5, Hardware Specifications.
