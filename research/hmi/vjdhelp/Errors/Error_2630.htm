Error 2630: [object name] [Switch] [property name] Momentary actions cannot occur after a Delay action.

There is a switch set up with either a Bit Momentary On or Momentary Off operation that occurs after a Delay operation.

Solution:

  • Momentary On/Off operations do not work after a Delay operation. Make sure operations occur in a logical sequence. Some operations must be the last operation in the list. For details, see Section 9.2, Creating Switches.
