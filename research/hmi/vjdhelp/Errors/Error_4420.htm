Error 4420: [action] [trigger type] [field name] Low Space event is not supported in the target without CF slot.

Low Space trigger actions only function on targets that support a CF card.

Solution:

  • Your target must support CF cards in order to trigger an action on Low Space. You may have to modify the action to be triggered in another way if your target does not support CF cards. For more information on Low Space, see Section 4.5.1, Target Properties.
