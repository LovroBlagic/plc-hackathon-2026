Error 2618: [Trend Graph] [object name] is too small to display the trend graph data. Increase the width and height.

The trend graph is too small to accurately display the defined number of samples.

Solution:

  • Increase the size of the trend graph.

  • As another solution, you can reduce the area occupied by labels. You can disable the DataAxisLabel altogether, reduce the font size, or reduce the number of digits displayed (DisplayRangeMin/DisplayRangeMax).
