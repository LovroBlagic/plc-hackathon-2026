5.1 Communications

To communicate with PLCs, inverters, and other equipment, connect them to either the target machine's serial port (RS-232C/RS-422), Ethernet port, or communication card/module, and then add a driver. Vijeo-Designer uses drivers to enable communications with equipment without you having to write complex communication programs.

Because of Vijeo-Designer's highly scalable communication system, you can connect multiple pieces of equipment to a single target machine. The equipment can be different models from different manufacturers and have different types of connections. Simply add the various drivers to your project.

  • Each target machine is set up with different communication ports. For information about the communication ports on your target machine, see Section 2.2, Supported Tablets, Smartphones, and Target Machines.
