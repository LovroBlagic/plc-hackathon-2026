5.3.2 Displaying Driver Statistics

Once you've created all your driver statistic variables, you can display the statistics in numeric displays, trend graphs, graphs or any other data display part. The following instructions use a pie graph to display driver statistics.

For a list of the available driver statistics, see Section 5.3, Collecting Driver Statistics: Statistic Tables.

  1. From the Toolchest, drag and drop one of the Pie Graphs onto the panel.

  1. In the Pie Graph properties, assign the driver statistic variables to the various channels.

In this example, we set up two variables —one for the number of successful (green) packets and one for the number of failed (orange) packets— then assigned them to different channels in the pie graph.
