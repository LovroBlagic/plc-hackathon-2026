5.8.4 FIPIO on XBTGT Series Fails to Connect to the Network

Communication failure may be caused by an invalid equipment type added to the PLC. PLCs configured with PL7 or Unity Pro software require the XBTGT equipment to be added to the PLC as:

Family: STD_P
Base Module: FED M32 EXTENDED DEV MOD 32W
Communicator: OTHER COM Other com

If the XBTGT equipment is added as XBTF equipment, communications will fail.
