The following table lists ASCII, ANSI and Vijeo Modern character codes from hex [00] to [FF].

European fonts support the defined characters for values 0x80 to 0xFF. Asian and Cyrillic fonts do not support character codes 0x80 to 0xFF. Vijeo-Modern fonts may not display some European accent characters properly.
