5.4.3 USB Cable Connections

Note the maximum cable length for USB 1.0 and 1.1 is 3 m (10 ft).

You can increase the maximum length of a cable segment for a USB 1.0 or 1.1 connection to 5 m (16.4 ft) by using a repeater (hub). Plus, you can connect up to 5 repeaters to increase the maximum overall length of USB cables to 27 m (88.5 ft).
