    • To use RS-232C in HMIBSU0ND1001, HMIBSUCND1W01, and HMIBSUSND1W01 target machines, go to Advanced BIOS Features Setup > Super IO Configuration in the target machine's BIOS settings and disable Auto Flow Control. For more information, refer to the S-Box iPC User Manual.

RS-232C

For all these target machines including the HMIG2U but not other HMIGTU Series target machines, connect serial equipment to the COM1 port with the Schneider Electric XBTZG9731 cable, or with a user-created cable following the connections in the diagram below.

For the HMIGTU Series (except HMIG2U), serial equipment can be connected to the COM2 port using the same type of cable.

    • Depending on the serial device you're using to link the CTS and RTS, pins may be required.

RS-422 (4-wire)

When making RS-422 (4-wire) connections on target machines, create the following cable and connect it to the appropriate port on the target machine. The RS-422 (4-wire) cable connections are for typical RS-422 serial equipment. Refer to the manual of your serial equipment for actual RS-422 (4-wire) cable connections.

All Target Machine series except for iPC (HMIPUx6, HMIPPx6, HMIBSU0ND1001, HMIBSUCND1W01, HMIBSUSND1W01 only)

 

    • Depending on the serial equipment you're connecting and the length of the cable, you may require termination resistance of 1/4W 330W on the target machine between RDA and RDB, and between SDA and SDB.
