*1 HMIGTU target machines support sound files that are 16-bit mono PCM or IMA ADPCM with 8, 12, or 16 kHz sampling rate. If you use a 11.025 kHz sound file on the HMIGTU, the file will play at 12 kHz.

*2 The supported mp4 standard is: container format ISO MPEG-4 (part 12), MPEG-4 Visual video codec with Simple L1 or Simple L2 profiles.

*3 HMIGTU target machines support only video of the MP4 file. At run time, the target machines will not play sound from the file.
