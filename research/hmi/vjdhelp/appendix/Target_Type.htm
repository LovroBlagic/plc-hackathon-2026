5.1.1 Target Types

Vijeo-Designer supports the following types of hardware:

  • Tablets/Smartphones

  • iPC Series

  • HMIGK Series

  • HMIGTO Series

  • HMIGTU Series

  • HMIGTUX Series

  • HMISCU Series

  • HMISTO Series

  • HMISTU Series

  • XBTGH Series

  • XBTGK Series

  • XBTGTW Series

  • XBTGC Series

  • XBTGT1005 Series

  • XBTGT2000 Series

  • XBTGT4000 Series

  • XBTGT5000 Series

  • XBTGT6000 Series

  • XBTGT7000 Series

  • New target machines (HMIGTO, HMIGK, HMIPEP, HMIPSP, HMIPSO, HMIBMU and HMIBMP) are only supported by the newest  versions of Vijeo Designer. If you have recently purchased new target machines, update your Vijeo Designer editor (build time) to ensure compatibility.

  • The XBTGC and HMISCU Series are only available when SoMachine is installed with Vijeo-Designer. If SoMachine is not installed, Vijeo-Designer will not display XBTGC and HMISCU as supported target types.
