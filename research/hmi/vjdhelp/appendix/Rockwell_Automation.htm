Rockwell Automation

Important Setup Information

For the following controllers that use RSLogix 5000 configuration software, the supported firmware version is 13 or later:

  • CompactLogix

  • ControlLogix

  • FlexLogix

This applies to all Rockwell protocols supported by Vijeo Designer (DF1-Full Duplex, DH485, and EtherNet/IP (including Explicit and Native)).
