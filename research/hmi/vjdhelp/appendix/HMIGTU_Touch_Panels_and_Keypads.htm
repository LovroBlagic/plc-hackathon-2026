* With certain limitations, projects created for HMIG5U/HMIG5U2/HMIG5U22 can still run on a Smart Display even when the project and the display have different resolutions. These limitations include:

    • A SVGA (800x600) project running on a XGA (1024x768) display will occupy the top left portion of the screen. The remainder of the screen will be filled with background color. The Windows environment will not be visible.

    • A XGA (1024x768) project running on a SVGA (800x600) display will extend beyond the screen as necessary. The extended areas of the project will not be visible and not be accessible to touch.

In any case, projects running on displays with non-matching resolutions will appear blurry. For best results, ensure that the project resolution matches the resolution of the connected display.
