11.9.5.1 Modifying a Keypad Part

  1. Click the Toolchest icon in the menu bar to open the Toolchest window, and click the Keypad folder.

  1. Move the keypad you want to use onto the panel using the drag and drop operation.

  1. Right-click the keypad on the panel, point to Group, and click Ungroup.

  1. Rearrange the keys and define the properties as required. For information on each setting, see the property descriptions in Section 11.9.5.2, Using Blank and Special Keys.
