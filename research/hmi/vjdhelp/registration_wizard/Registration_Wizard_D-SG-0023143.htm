Introduction
The behavior of unregistered Schneider Electric software products depends on the kind of software product and version you are using.
In principle the software product behavior varies according to the following types of registration:
  • mandatory registration
  • optional registration
Mandatory Registration
The software product must be registered until the trial period has expired, i.e. when a given time limit or launch limit has been reached. When the trial period is expired, the software product will either stop working, or continue working with a limited functionality.
NOTE: For continuous use of your software product, please register before the time or launch limit expires.
Optional Registration
The software product runs without a time limit or launch limit. But at certain launch or time intervals a registration reminder dialog will be displayed.
