Introduction
You can transfer your license to another person or company. Transferring the license to another person or company is just a procedure for updating the licensee information in the SRC database and the owner information of your software product.
Transferring the license to another person requires various steps in the following sequence:
  1. change the licensee information and send the new information to the SRC
  2. transfer the license rights to another PC
    Note: This is an optional procedure which is described in the information before, see Basics about Transferring License Rights.
Transfer Procedure
To transfer the license to another person or company, perform the following steps:
Step
Action
1
Start the Registration Wizard, see Starting the Registration Wizard.
Result:
The wizard’s Select Task dialog box is displayed.
2
Select Transfer License Rights, and then click Next.
Result:
The wizard’s Select Transfer Task dialog box is displayed.
3
Select Transfer the license to another person, and then click Next.
Result:
The wizard’s Select Schneider Electric Contact Method dialog box is displayed.
4
Select the desired method, either By phone, By fax or By e-mail, and then click Next.
Result:
The wizard’s Current Licensee dialog box is displayed.
5
Check and if needed correct the required license and user information of the current licensee, click Print if you need the information for further use, and then click Next.
Note: You need to check the user information and correct them if necessary.
Result:
The wizard’s New Licensee dialog box is displayed.
6
Enter the required license and user information of the new licensee, click Print if you need the information for further use, and then click Next.
Result:
If you have selected
  • By phone, the wizard's Call Registration Center dialog box is displayed, showing the phone numbers to be used.
  • By fax, the wizard's Select Fax Number dialog box is displayed, showing the fax numbers to be used.
  • By e-mail, the wizard's Select E-mail Address dialog box is displayed, showing the e-mail addresses to be used.
7
If you have selected
  • By phone, call Schneider Electric to communicate the information, and then click Next.
  • By fax, select 1 of the fax numbers, and then click Next.
  • By e-mail, select 1 of the e-mail addresses, and then click Next.
Result:
The wizard’s Exit the Registration Wizard dialog box is displayed.
8
Click Finish to close the Registration Wizard.
9
If you have selected
  • By phone, you can print the displayed form to keep the information for your files.
  • By fax, print and send the fax form to the SRC.
  • By e-mail, the procedure has been completed in step 9.
10
Optional step
You can transfer the software product license rights to another PC, if necessary, see also Transfer License Rights from this PC to another PC.
