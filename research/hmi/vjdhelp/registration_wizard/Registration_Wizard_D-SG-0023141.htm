Introduction
The privacy statement explains our policy regarding any personal information you may supply to us when register Schneider Electric software products.
At certain times, we may need information from you and may ask you to complete a registration form. Our goal in collecting this personal information during the software product registration process is threefold:
  • to provide you with the option to register your Schneider Electric software product to your convenience,
  • to allow you to receive the necessary updates and notices for effective use of your software product, and
  • to protect both you and our software product from those who would use our software product fraudulently.
NOTE: The more you tell us about you during the registration process, the more able we are to deliver or allow you to access the most relevant information that meets your needs.
Registration by Web
To extend our service, we may occasionally save information on your PC in the form of a cookie. Thus, all cookie features must be enabled when you register your software product by web or by web on another PC.
Commitment
Schneider Electric will neither share e-mail addresses with persons outside Schneider Electric nor disclose any user information to any third parties.
NOTE: At any time, you have the option to remove your information from the Schneider Electric databases if you no longer wish to have this information used as a basis for further contact with you. In this case, please send an e-mail to the SRC.
Thank you for choosing a Schneider Electric software product. We appreciate your business.
