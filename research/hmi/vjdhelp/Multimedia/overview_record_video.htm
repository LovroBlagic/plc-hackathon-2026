19.1.4.1 Recording Live Video

Types of Video recording:

  • Start/Stop Recording
    Records the live video stream directly to a video file on a storage device. See Section 19.1.4.2, Setting up Start/Stop Video Recording.

  • Buffer Recording
    Continually records the live video feed to the target's buffer. The buffer size can be configured to store between 0.5 to 10 minutes of recorded video. When the user wants to view the video buffer, they can flush the buffer's recorded video to a video file on a storage device and then playback the video file. See Section 19.1.4.3, Setting up Buffer Video Recording.

Resolution

  • XBTGT Series target machines can record video up to a maximum of 320x240 pixel resolution.

  • Other target machine series recorded video resolution depends on the recording equipment's supported resolution.

  • Different target machine series record video in different formats. See Appendix 5 Hardware Specifications for the format your target records video in.

  • If you are using Buffer Recording, make sure you have enough space available for the video buffer. See Section 25.3.3, Checking Memory Usage.
