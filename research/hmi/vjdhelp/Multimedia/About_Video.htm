19.1.1 About Video

Vijeo-Designer provides you with a customizable Video Display part that can display and record the live video feed coming from the target's camera. For a list of target machines that support video, see Appendix 5, Hardware Specifications.

With Video you can:

  • Display Live Video from the target's camera.

  • Record Live Video from the target camera's video feed.

  • Playback Video recorded on the target or play a video added to the target.

  • Take Video Snapshots and either save to file or print.

  • Use the Image Capture Viewer to display screen captures or video snapshots.

  • Vijeo-Designer Runtime will only allow one Playback and one Live Video Display to show video at the same time.

  • You cannot record live video in one Video Display and at the same time play a recorded video file in another Video Display.

  • The Bad Data Quality is not supported for the Video Display.

  • Video Displays are not supported on popup windows.

Video Formats

Different target series record and play different video formats. See Appendix 5, Hardware Specifications for the types of video formats your target machine will play and record in.

 

  • Make sure your video files are supported by testing the video file in the Video Display on the target machine.

NTSC and PAL

NTSC and PAL are the two standard video signals supported. Make sure the video signal you select matches your camera's video signal.
