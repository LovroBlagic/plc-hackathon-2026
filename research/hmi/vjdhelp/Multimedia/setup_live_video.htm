19.1.3.2 Setting up Live Video

Follow the steps below to display live video in your application.

  1. In the Target properties, enable video by selecting NTSC or PAL. See Section 19.1.1, About Video: NTSC and PAL.

  2. Draw the Video Display. See Section19.1.2, Drawing a Video Display.

  3. In the Video Display properties, select Display Live Feed. See Section 19.1.8.1, General Tab.

  4. Configure the Video settings as required by your project. See Section 19.1.7, Video Settings.
