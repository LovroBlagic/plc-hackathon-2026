19.1.9.1 Keypad Video Controls

The table below describes the individual Video Display keypad buttons.

Button

Button Name

Description

Play

Plays the video.

Stop

Stops the current video operation.

Pause

Pauses the live video feed or pauses the recorded video playing in the Video Display part.

Rewind

Rewinds the recorded video until the Stop or Play button is touched.

Repeatedly pressing the Rewind button increases the rewinding speed.

Fast Forward

Fast forwards the recorded video until the Stop or Play button is touched.

Repeatedly pressing the Fast Forward button increases the speed of the fast forward.

Record

Records the video feed to a file.

Snapshot

Takes a snapshot of the video showing in the Video Display part and stores the snapshot to a file.

Progress Bar

Indicates the following for these keypads:

  • Play Video keypad
    The Progress Bar indicates the progress of the recorded video displaying in the Video Display part. Touch the area on the Progress Bar where you want to start the video playback.

  • Select File keypad
    The Progress Bar is a short cut for selecting a recorded video to play from the list of available recorded videos. Touch the area on the Progress Bar where the recorded video you want to play is located.

Full Screen / Restore Screen

Resizes the Video Display part to occupy the entire panel. Touch anywhere on the Video Display to restore it back to its original size.

Video Display Settings

Depending on the target type, the button will display either the Settings keypad or the Settings menu of the camera connected to the target machine. See Appendix 5, Hardware Specifications, for the list of the targets that support these settings.

For information on the Settings keypad, see Section 19.1.9.1, Keypad Video Controls: Settings Keypad.

  • If a video camera is not connected to the target machine and the target machine is supposed to display the camera's settings menu, then the button will not be supported.

Up

Moves the selection up in the recorded video files list.

Down

Moves the selection down in the recorded video files list.

Close

Closes the keypad.

Live Video / Play Video

Toggles the Video Display between live video and play video. Click the Close button after clicking live video button to start this feature.

OK

Initializes the selected video file to play.

Folder

Displays the Select File Keypad.
