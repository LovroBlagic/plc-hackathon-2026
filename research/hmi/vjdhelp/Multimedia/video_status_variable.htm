19.1.10.2 Video Record Status Variable

The video variable, _VideoRecord.Status, stores the value that corresponds to the status of the video recording operation.

Bit

Value

Operation Name

Description

0

0 or 1

Idle

No operation.

1

2

Error

An error has occurred during the video operation.

2

4

Recording

Live video feed is being recorded.

3

8

Playing

A recorded video is playing in a Video Display.

4

16

Pause

The video is paused.

5

32

Fast Forward

Recorded video is fast forwarding.

6

64

Rewind

Recorded video is rewinding.
