19.1.8 Video Display Settings

To display the Video Display Settings:

  • Double-click the Video Display or draw a Video Display to show its properties. See Section 19.1.2, Drawing a Video Display.

The following table provides an overview of the Video Display Settings tabs.

Tab

Settings

Section

General Tab

The General Tab defines:

  • The type of video played by the Video Display: Live Feed or Recorded Video

  • Popup Keypads for the Video Display.
