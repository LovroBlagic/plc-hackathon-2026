19.1.5.1 Playing Video Recordings Overview

The Video Display can be configured to play:

  • A single recorded video

  • Any recorded video defined by a string expression

  • Any recorded video the user selects on the Video keypad

Resolution

  • You can only play video files with 320x240 pixel resolution on XBTGT Series target machines.

  • You cannot play and record videos at the same time on targets.

  • You will not be able to playback recorded videos on the target machine if you set Data Files to Main Drive and Run Time Data Location to Secondary or Optional drive in the Data Locations settings. For more information, see Appendix, 5.11.1, Types of Data Locations.
