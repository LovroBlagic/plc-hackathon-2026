19.1.3.1 Live Video

The live video feed streams from the target's video camera and into the target machine. The feed can be either real-time video being filmed on the video camera or a recorded video being played on the video camera.

  • Targets cannot play the sound from a live video feed. However, when live video is recorded, the live video's sound is recorded to the video file. Targets can play the sound for recorded videos. All targets that support video also support sound.
