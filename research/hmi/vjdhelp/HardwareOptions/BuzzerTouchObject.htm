24.3.2 Enable a Touch Animated Object's Buzzer

With touch animation, you can enable an object to output a buzzer sound when the object has been touched.

  1. Double-click an object to open the Animation Properties dialog box.

  1. Click the Touch tab and then click the Enable Touch Animation box. Set up the object's touch animation.
    For details, see Chapter 8, Animating Graphics.

  1. Select Buzzer On Touch and then click OK.
