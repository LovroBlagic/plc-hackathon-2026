25.6.1.3 Downloading Through a Firewall

When the target machine and editor PC reside on opposite sides of a firewall, you may need to configure your firewall.

The editor PC and target machine use FTP commands to download, upload, and transfer data. In order to allow free flow of Vijeo-Designer FTP commands through the firewall, configure the firewall to allow FTP commands using ports 6001 and 13777.

When the firewall is configured correctly, you can download and upload user applications and transfer data over the network, through a firewall, to and from the target machine.
